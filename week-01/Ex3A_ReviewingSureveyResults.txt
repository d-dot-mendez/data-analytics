5. Now that you can see the responses, how might you recommend rephrasing this or
question or the response options in a future survey? 
I would split some questions into two. For example, "do you have an external keyboard and mouse" would become "Do you have an eternal keyboard?", "Do you have a mouse?"
I would ask "Which do you think is the best thing to do five minutes before class?" And have multiple choice options. It does remove a lot of insight by having the participants choose an answer rather than coming up with one on their own, but I would prefer that since the system does not yet know how to differentiate a participant restating their answer from the participant actually giving their answer.
